# Bright Smile Dental — Services (Sample Data)

Bright Smile Dental Clinic offers general and cosmetic dentistry for families in the fictional town of Maple Grove.

## Preventive care

- **New patient exam and cleaning** — $129 (sample price). Includes X-rays for most adults.
- **Routine cleaning (every 6 months)** — $89 for established patients.
- **Fluoride treatment (children)** — $35.

## Restorative

- **Composite filling (1 surface)** — from $165.
- **Crown (porcelain)** — from $980; we provide a written estimate after exam.

## Cosmetic

- **Professional whitening (in-office)** — $450 per session.
- **Take-home whitening kit** — $199.

## Emergency

We reserve same-day slots for **tooth pain, swelling, or broken teeth**. Call **(555) 014-7721** before 2:00 PM Mon–Fri for same-day availability.

## What we do not offer

We do **not** provide orthodontic braces, oral surgery, or sleep-apnea appliances. We refer those cases to partner specialists.
