# Insurance & Billing FAQ (Sample Data)

## Do you accept insurance?

We are **in-network** with these fictional plans for demo purposes:

- Maple Health PPO
- River Valley Dental HMO
- Summit Care Open Access

Bring your member ID to your visit. We file claims on your behalf.

## Payment without insurance

We accept cash, credit cards, and CarePlan financing (subject to approval). Payment is due at time of service unless a prior arrangement is documented.

## Cancellation policy

Please cancel or reschedule at least **24 hours** in advance. Late cancellations may incur a **$50** fee.

## Records requests

Email **records@brightsmile-demo.example** with your name and date of birth. Allow 3 business days for copies.
