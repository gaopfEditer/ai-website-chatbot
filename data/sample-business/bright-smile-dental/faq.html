How do I book an appointment?

Call (555) 014-7721 or use our online form at brightsmile-demo.example/book (demo link only).

Do you see children?

Yes. We welcome patients age 3 and older. Younger children may be referred to a pediatric specialist.

Is whitening safe for sensitive teeth?

We assess sensitivity during a consult. Mild sensitivity is common for 24–48 hours after in-office whitening.

Do you offer dental implants?

No. Implant placement is referred to Maple Grove Oral Surgery (partner).
